Exhibit D · Luck Lab · Honors thesis

Does electrode placement change what EEG can decode?

EEG records brain activity with scalp electrodes. A decoder is a machine-learning model that guesses what a person was looking at from that activity. High-density caps cost more and take longer to set up, so I asked whether fewer electrodes, or different placement, would hurt decoding.

E

Exhibit E · Python · scikit-learn · TensorFlow

Predicting sleep disorders

A supervised machine-learning project that predicts whether a person has insomnia, sleep apnea or no sleep disorder from lifestyle and health data. I compared three models, and the support vector classifier did best.

Test accuracy by model
The data

374 people from a Kaggle sleep health and lifestyle dataset: 219 with no disorder, 78 with sleep apnea, 77 with insomnia.

Features include age, gender, occupation, sleep duration, activity level, stress, BMI category, blood pressure, heart rate and daily steps.

Gender, occupation, sleep duration and age stood out as important indicators across the models.
